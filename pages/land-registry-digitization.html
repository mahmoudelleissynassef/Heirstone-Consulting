Land Registry & Property Transaction Digitization

Secure, searchable title is the precondition for every mortgage market, every land tax base and every credible investment offer. We advise governments on modernising land registries and property transaction services — reconciling cadastral and legal records, digitising the transfer journey, and building the institutional arrangements that keep the register trustworthy over time.

Our Methodology

Records & Tenure Baseline

We assess the register and the cadastre together: coverage, accuracy, the volume of unregistered or informally held parcels, and the extent of divergence between the legal record and physical occupation. This determines whether the mandate is systematic first registration, a data migration, or a service-layer modernisation — three very different programmes.

Legal & Institutional Framework

Digital title requires legal standing. We identify the amendments needed for electronic records, digital signature and evidentiary weight to hold, and clarify the mandates of registry, survey, judicial and municipal bodies so that accountability for the integrity of the register is unambiguous.

Transaction Journey & Data Architecture

We redesign the transfer, mortgage and subdivision journeys end to end — including valuation, tax and notarial steps — and define the parcel identifier, data model and integrations to identity and payment systems that allow a transaction to complete without the same evidence being re-presented at every counter.

Rollout, Funding & Value Capture

We phase implementation against institutional capacity and cost, and build the business case around what the register unlocks: mortgage lending, a defensible land and property tax base, faster investor land access, and reliable evidence for planning. Funding and cost-recovery options are structured alongside the delivery plan.
