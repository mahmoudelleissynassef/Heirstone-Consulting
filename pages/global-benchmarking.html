Global Benchmarking

We compare a country, city or sector with the places it competes with and the places it wants to become, so that targets and policies rest on evidence rather than ambition alone. Each benchmark is built for the decision it informs.

Our Methodology

Peer Selection

We choose comparators by the question being asked: direct competitors, aspirational leaders, and places that started from similar conditions. A short, well-chosen set of peers says more than a long league table.

Indicators and Data

We define the indicators that matter for the decision, from visitor numbers, length of stay and spend to land supply, investment flows and regulatory steps, and assemble comparable data from official statistics, operators and our own datasets.

Case Studies and Lessons

For the most relevant peers we go beyond the numbers to how results were achieved: the institutions, policies, incentives, sequencing and mistakes. Each case ends with the lessons that can, and cannot, be transferred.

Targets and Gap Analysis

We turn the benchmark into realistic targets and the gaps to close, with the measures, timeframes and resources each gap requires, ready to feed a strategy, a business case or a monitoring framework.
