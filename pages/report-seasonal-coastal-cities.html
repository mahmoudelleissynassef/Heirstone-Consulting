Seasonal Coastal Cities: Beyond August

What turns a resort coast into a year-round city, measured across 31 resort towns, and what it means for Egypt's North Coast, Ras El Hekma and the Saudi Red Sea.

In brief

A resort coast's winter is largely settled before its first home is sold.

We measured seasonality at 20 resort airports in Europe and Türkiye and in 12 coastal regions, and the housing of 31 resort towns. The coasts that work all year have two things the others lack: homes that are lived in and a winter market that arrives by air. Most also have a large employer that stays open in February. We apply the findings to Egypt's North Coast, New Alamein, Ras El Hekma and the Saudi Red Sea.

  1. Airport counts understate how seasonal a resort economy is.

    Our Seasonality Index divides a year's air passengers by those of the busiest month: Mykonos's year is worth 4.0 peak months and Lanzarote's 10.8. Crete's tourist nights in August 2025 were 78.6 times January's, against 14.3 times at Heraklion airport, and its bed-places were 94% used in August and 1.2% in January.

  2. Winter demand comes from abroad, and that is where growth has been.

    In the Algarve, domestic guests' nights swing 11.6 times between January and August and foreign guests' 4.0 times, and foreigners supply 80.9% of November to March nights. Between 2014 and 2024 Faro's November to March passengers rose 98.6%, twice the growth of its summer, and Malaga's January traffic rose 128%.

  3. The housing mix a resort starts with tends to stay.

    La Grande-Motte had 2.47 dwellings per resident in 1975 and 2.46 in 2023, while Saint-Tropez has lost 42% of its residents since 1968. Of 11 French resorts, only the two inside an urban corridor have a majority of homes lived in all year, and second-home surcharges and caps came after the shares were already high.

  4. Few anchors move January, and the popular ones are small.

    Large universities, state-funded winter travel and a big city within daily reach go with fuller winters, and only the state-funded travel has a directly measured effect. Spain's 27,875 digital-nomad permit holders equal about a ninth of Marbella's estimated summer influx, and Antalya's foreign residents fell by about a quarter within a year of their 2022 peak.

  5. Egypt is turning its North Coast into year-round cities.

    New Alamein already has a university, an industrial zone and the cabinet's summer meetings, and Ras El Hekma is planned for over one million people. High-speed rail is due to reach the coast from July 2027 and desalination capacity is set to rise 2.6 times by 2028. EU-Egypt air traffic already flies 38.2% of its passengers in November to March, and bringing that winter market to the Mediterranean shore is the next step.

What it means

Recommendations by audience

Governments and land agencies

Fix the housing mix, the first institutions and the phasing of utilities in the masterplan, and publish monthly data on airports, hotels and utilities.

Master developers

Report January occupancy alongside sales and lead each phase with hotels, campuses or clinics that stay open all year.

Investors and lenders

Underwrite on the November to March share of demand and treat incentive-led and event-led demand as cyclical.

Hotel operators

Contract shoulder and winter seats with airlines and tour operators early, and coordinate winter opening across the destination.

Frequently asked questions

Why do resort coasts empty out after the summer?

Because most rely on a domestic summer market, with homes used as second homes and no winter market that arrives by air. Crete's tourist nights in August 2025 were 78.6 times January's, and its bed-places were 1.2% used in January.

What makes a coastal city work all year?

Homes that are lived in, a winter market that arrives by air and, usually, a large employer such as a university that stays open in February. In the Algarve, foreign guests supply 80.9% of November to March nights, and resort cities with a large campus enrol the equivalent of 2 to 4% of their residents.

What does this mean for Egypt's North Coast and Ras El Hekma?

Egypt is turning the North Coast into year-round cities, with Ras El Hekma planned for over one million people and high-speed rail due from July 2027. What remains is a published housing mix, a winter market and utilities phased to occupancy, and EU-Egypt air traffic already flies 38.2% of its passengers in November to March.
