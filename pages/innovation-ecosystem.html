Innovation Ecosystem Strategy

We support the design of comprehensive frameworks for the growth of vibrant tech, innovation, and creative economies.

Our Methodology

Ecosystem Diagnosis & Benchmarking

Assessing the current state of the innovation ecosystem across key pillars: talent, capital, policy, infrastructure, and culture.

Sector Focus & Niche Identification

Identifying specific technology or creative industry verticals where the city or nation can build a competitive advantage.

Strategic Initiative & Program Design

Designing a portfolio of targeted initiatives, such as incubators, venture funds, talent programs, and policy reforms.

Governance & Performance Metrics

Establishing a governance model to coordinate ecosystem stakeholders and defining KPIs to measure progress and impact.
