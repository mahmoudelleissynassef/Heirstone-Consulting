Visitor Economy

We help destinations understand and grow the value that visitors bring: where they go, what they spend, who benefits and where the gaps are. The result is a plan that turns more visits into more jobs, income and investment.

Our Methodology

Visitor Flows and Spending

We map visitor flows and spending across the destination by segment, season and place, using arrival data, card spending, surveys and mobility data where available.

Economic Impact

We estimate the direct, indirect and induced effects of visitor spending on output, jobs and tax revenue, and show which sectors and communities capture the benefit and where it leaks out.

Opportunity and Gap Analysis

We identify what holds the visitor economy back, such as access, seasonality, product gaps, skills or regulation, and the opportunities to raise length of stay, spending and the spread of visitors.

Visitor Economy Strategy

We bring the findings into a strategy with priorities, projects, partnerships and targets, linked to the destination's tourism, investment and land-use plans and backed by a monitoring framework.
