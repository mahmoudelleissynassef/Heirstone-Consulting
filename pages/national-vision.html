National Vision

We help governments turn a national vision into a strategy that can be delivered: clear priorities for real estate, tourism and economic diversification, the institutions to lead them, and the investment needed to get there.

Our Methodology

Diagnostic and Baseline

We assess the economy's starting point sector by sector: competitive strengths, constraints, institutions, regulation and investment flows, and how the country compares with its peers.

Vision and Strategic Pillars

With leadership and stakeholders, we define the long-term ambition and the few pillars that will deliver it, each with objectives, measurable targets and the sectors and places where change will happen first.

Programmes and Projects

We translate the pillars into programmes and flagship projects with scope, cost, phasing and funding routes, including what the private sector can deliver through partnerships and what the state must lead.

Delivery and Governance

We set out the governance, delivery units and monitoring framework, and the early wins that build momentum, so that the vision survives changes in leadership and the pressures of day-to-day government.
